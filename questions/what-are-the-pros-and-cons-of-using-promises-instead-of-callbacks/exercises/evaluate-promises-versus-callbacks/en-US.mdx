Which statements correctly describe Promises compared with callback APIs? Select all that apply.

## Options

### one-eventual-outcome (correct)

A Promise standardizes one eventual fulfillment or rejection.

> This avoids callback contracts that might otherwise be ambiguous about call count and error routing.

### composable-flow (correct)

Promise chains, `async`/`await`, and combinators compose sequential and parallel asynchronous outcomes.

> Returned values and errors propagate through a consistent abstraction.

### repeated-events

A single Promise is the natural representation for an unbounded stream of repeated events.

> A Promise settles once; event listeners, async iterables, or observable streams fit repeated values better.

### built-in-cancellation

A Promise automatically cancels its underlying operation when no `.then()` handlers remain.

> Cancellation needs an explicit operation-level mechanism.

### caller-must-return-await (correct)

Callers must return or await promise work to preserve error propagation and completion tracking.

> Dropping a promise can create early completion or an unhandled rejection.
