A logging function accepts `environment`, `service`, `level`, and `message`. Explain how currying or partial application could improve its use without claiming it is always clearer.

## Answer

Partially apply stable context such as environment and service to produce a service-specific logger, then optionally curry or expose a second function that fixes the level before accepting a message. Call sites become shorter and cannot repeatedly supply inconsistent context, while the general function remains reusable. Keep the direct multi-argument form if arguments are rarely reused or the chain obscures parameter meaning; the benefit comes from meaningful staged configuration, not from maximizing the number of returned functions.
