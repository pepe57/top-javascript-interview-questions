Which statements about Promise state are correct? Select all that apply.

## Options

### three-states (correct)

A promise is pending, fulfilled with a value, or rejected with a reason.

> Fulfilled and rejected are the two mutually exclusive settled outcomes.

### settlement-is-final (correct)

After a promise settles, later attempts to fulfill or reject it do not change its state.

> Promise settlement is a one-way transition from pending to one settled outcome.

### resolution-can-adopt-pending (correct)

A promise resolved with another pending promise can remain pending while adopting the other promise’s eventual state.

> Resolution describes locking in an outcome source, not necessarily immediate fulfillment.

### resolved-means-fulfilled

“Resolved” always means the promise is already fulfilled with a non-promise value.

> A resolved promise can be following another promise that has not settled yet.

### rejected-can-recover-same-promise

Calling `.catch()` changes the original rejected promise back to fulfilled.

> `.catch()` returns a new promise; it does not mutate the state of the original promise.
