A `Report` can optionally be encrypted, cached, compressed, and retried. Explain whether a subclass for every capability combination or composition is the better default, and when inheritance would still be appropriate.

## Answer

Composition is the better default because the capabilities are optional and independently variable; injecting or wrapping strategies avoids a rapidly growing subclass hierarchy and lets each capability change independently. Inheritance is appropriate when a subtype has a stable is-a relationship, honors the parent contract everywhere it is used, and needs a coherent specialization rather than a mix of unrelated optional behaviors.

## Checklist

- Recognizes the capabilities as independently variable concerns.
- Explains how composition avoids a combinatorial subclass hierarchy.
- Limits inheritance to a stable substitutable is-a relationship.
