Which statements about Promises are correct? Select all that apply.

## Options

### settles-once (correct)

A promise moves from pending to one fulfilled or rejected outcome and cannot later switch outcomes.

> Subsequent resolve or reject attempts are ignored after settlement.

### methods-return-new-promises (correct)

`.then()`, `.catch()`, and `.finally()` return new promises.

> This lets values, errors, and asynchronous results compose through a chain.

### reactions-are-microtasks (correct)

Reaction callbacks run as microtasks even when their source promise is already settled.

> They never run synchronously inside the `.then()` registration call.

### moves-work-thread

Wrapping computation in `new Promise()` automatically moves that computation to another thread.

> The executor runs synchronously on the calling thread unless it explicitly invokes another host mechanism.

### automatic-cancellation

Every promise exposes a built-in method that cancels its underlying operation.

> Cancellation requires an operation-specific mechanism such as `AbortSignal` and cannot be inferred from the promise alone.
