Which statements accurately describe scope setup and declaration evaluation? Select all that apply.

## Options

### var-starts-undefined (correct)

A `var` binding is initialized to `undefined` before the scope's statements execute.

> Scope instantiation initializes `var`; only its initializer or later assignment waits for statement evaluation.

### lexical-bindings-do-not-exist

Bindings declared with `let` and `const` do not exist until evaluation reaches their declarations.

> The bindings are created when the block is instantiated but remain uninitialized in the temporal dead zone.

### lexical-bindings-start-uninitialized (correct)

Bindings declared with `let` and `const` remain uninitialized until their declarations are evaluated.

> This uninitialized state is why any early read of either binding throws `ReferenceError`.

### initializers-run-during-setup

Initializer expressions for `var`, `let`, and `const` all run during scope instantiation.

> Initializers are evaluated at their source locations, after scope setup has created the bindings.
