Which pairing best matches the desired behavior?

## Options

### debounce-search-throttle-scroll (correct)

Debounce an autocomplete request until typing pauses; throttle a scroll-position update to run at a limited rate while scrolling continues.

> Autocomplete usually wants the final settled input, while scroll feedback often needs periodic updates during continuous activity.

### throttle-search-debounce-scroll

Throttle autocomplete so every interval sends an intermediate query; debounce scroll rendering until scrolling stops.

> That reverses the common requirements: it sends avoidable queries and removes ongoing scroll feedback.

### debounce-both-immediate

Debounce both operations because debouncing guarantees an immediate first call and regular calls afterward.

> A typical trailing debounce waits until calls stop; it does not guarantee regular execution during activity.

### throttle-both-final-only

Throttle both operations because throttling runs only once after the final call.

> Throttling limits execution frequency during a stream; final-only behavior describes a trailing debounce.
